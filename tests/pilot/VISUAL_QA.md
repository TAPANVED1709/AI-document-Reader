# Synthetic PDF visual check

All 31 documents (50 pages) were inspected during Phase 14: contact sheets covered each first page and all 19 continuation pages. Enlarged Poppler renders checked the parallel liver/kidney panels, sex-specific printed ranges, faded decimal scans and the added triplet/Absent fixture. PyMuPDF renders were used for the contact sheets.

Observed: readable titles and row spacing, separated parallel panels, three-column test/value/reference layouts, visible wrapped names/range continuations, correct page progression, and no clipping or patient identifiers. The faint decimal marks and I/l name substitutions are intentional challenge damage, not layout defects to erase. Lower-resolution scans remain image-only; hybrid PDFs retain native pages around the scanned page.

The check was performed by the development agent. It is visual engineering QA, not independent qualified laboratory adjudication. The frozen fixture PDFs and truth manifest are the retained evidence. Temporary renders remain under tmp/pdfs/phase14 as locally ignored QA working files because automatic approval review blocked the cleanup command.
